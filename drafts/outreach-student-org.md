## Why LixBlogs fits — the engineering case

Lead with the 3–4 that matter most; cut the rest.

- **One credible home for everything you publish.** A dedicated organization page and
  profile at a clean URL you control — statements, explainers, reports — not scattered
  across feeds that bury them.
- **Identity-shielded posting ("secret mode").** Publish with a pseudonym or no public
  byline; secret-mode posts are de-indexed from search and get restricted sharing/embedding
  controls. *(Public pseudonymity — account records are retained server-side; see the
  honesty note in the email. Not legal anonymity.)*
- **A team, not one login.** Real organization roles — **admin, maintainer, writer,
  reader** — so coordinators, writers, and members each get the right access, all
  publishing under one banner.
- **Collections for campaigns.** Group posts into named series ("Updates," "Explainers,"
  "Ground Reports") so readers can follow a thread instead of hunting a timeline.
- **Write together.** Co-authoring, with credit for everyone who contributed.
- **Built for real content.** A modern block editor — headings, images, code, math,
  diagrams, tables, callouts, auto table of contents.
- **Credible when shared.** Every post generates a clean, professional share card for
  WhatsApp / X / LinkedIn / Discord — a shared link that carries weight.
- **Fast everywhere, scales quietly.** Global edge hosting; stays fast from ten readers to
  ten thousand, and you never touch a server.
- **Free, and we host it.** No infrastructure or cost on your side — we handle setup,
  hosting, upkeep, and scaling.

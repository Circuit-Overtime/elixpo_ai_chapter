This is the original breadboard connection notebook. The schematic and README
are the authoritative wiring references for programmer v10.

This project is a 28C64 EEPROM programmer using an Arduino Nano and two
74HC595 shift registers.

here's the connections on the breadboard  arduino to i/o

d12 - i/o 7 
d11 - i/0 6
d10 - i/0 5
d9 - i/0 4
d8 - i/0 3
d7 - i/0 2
d6 - i/0 1
d5 - i/0 0

OE - A0
WE - D13
CE - A3 with 10k pull-up to +5v (not directly to GND)
PWR GND connected

here's the connection of the hc595s

frist hc595 
has pwr and gnd
qh' NC
SRCLR +5v 
SRCLK - TIED SHORT WITH SRCLK of 2nd hc595 D3 of arduino
RCLK - TIED SHORT WITH RCLK of 2nd HC595 D4 arduino
OE - GND
SER - QH' second HC595
qa - A8 of 28C64
qb - A9 of 28C64
qc - A10 of 28C64
qd - A11 of 28C64
qe - A12 of 28C64
qf - 
qg
qh

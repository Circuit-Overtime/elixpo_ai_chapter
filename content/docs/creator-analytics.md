Open **Settings → Stats** to understand reach, reading quality, audience, acquisition, and growth for your personal profile or an organization you are authorized to manage.

## Choose a scope and range

Select the publishing scope first, then choose a preset or custom date range. Comparison values use the preceding equivalent period. Keep the same scope and range when comparing cards, charts, tables, and exports.

## Core metrics

- **Views and unique visitors** describe reach.
- **Reads, average read time, and reading depth** describe consumption quality.
- **Completion and engagement rates** turn activity into comparable percentages.
- **Followers gained and lost** show audience growth.
- **Acquisition and audience charts** explain sources, devices, geography, and returning readers when enough data exists.

Analytics can be incomplete for older periods because dimensional collection began after basic historical counters. Privacy controls, blocked scripts, connectivity, and deduplication can also make analytics differ from raw page requests.

## Export

Use **CSV** for analysis in a spreadsheet. Use **PNG** to export the visual report for presentations or updates. Review the selected scope and dates before sharing an export.

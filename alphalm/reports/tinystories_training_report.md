# TinyStories Model Training Report (v5.5.4)

This report documents the training process, hyperparameters, datasets, and final validation/test metrics for each language component trained on the extracted 1MB TinyStories subset.

---

## 1. Dataset Extraction Summary
* **Source Corpus**: `C:/Users/user/Desktop/AI projects/1-Bit LLM/tinystories.txt` (~1.79 GB)
* **Subset File**: `tinystories_1m.txt` (~1.01 MB, complete stories only)
* **Statistics**:
  * **Complete Stories**: 1,088
  * **Sentences**: 21,882 (filtered to length >= 4 tokens for training)
  * **Words**: 198,629 (cleaned/tokenized)
  * **Average Story Length**: 20.1 sentences / 182.6 words
  * **Average Sentence Length**: 9.1 words

## 2. Word2Vec Embeddings
A narrative-focused Word2Vec embedding space was trained from scratch.
* **Hyperparameters**:
  * `vector_size`: 128
  * `window`: 5
  * `min_count`: 1
  * `epochs`: 20
  * `seed`: 42
* **Outcome**:
  * Vocabulary Size: 9,010 unique words mapped.
  * Checkpoint Saved: `models/tinystories_word2vec.model`

## 3. Sentence Validity Head
Trained to distinguish grammatically valid TinyStories sentences from 8 corruption types.
* **Architecture**: Bidirectional GRU (hidden size 64) + Global Max Pooling + Late concatenation with 7 handcrafted features (length_char, num_tokens, punctuation_count, unique_token_ratio, repeated_bigram_count, seen_bigram_fraction, is_perfect_bigram).
* **Dataset**: Balanced split of 20,188 positives and 20,188 corrupted negatives.
  * Train: 32,300 samples
  * Val: 4,038 samples
  * Test: 4,038 samples
* **Training Hyperparameters**:
  * Epochs: 15 (Early stopping saved epoch 14 best)
  * Learning Rate: 1e-4 (embedding), 1e-3 (GRU and MLP)
  * Batch Size: 64
* **Final Test Metrics**:
  * **Accuracy**: `91.28%`
  * **Precision**: `87.56%`
  * **Recall**: `96.24%`
  * **F1 Score**: `0.9169`
  * **ROC AUC**: `0.9593`
* **Checkpoint Saved**: `models/validity_tinystories.pt`

---

## 4. Makes-Sense Head
Trained to rank natural sequential story segments above corrupted transitions.
* **Architecture**: Bidirectional GRU (hidden size 128) + 2-layer MLP classifier mapping trajectories of sizes 3, 4, 5, 6 to a [0, 1] coherence score. Trained with Margin Ranking Loss (margin = 0.1).
* **Dataset**: Balanced sliding window trajectories.
  * Train: 57,836 samples
  * Val: 7,230 samples
  * Test: 7,230 samples
* **Training Hyperparameters**:
  * Epochs: 15 (Early stopping saved epoch 15 best)
  * Learning Rate: 1e-3 with plateau decay
  * Batch Size: 64
* **Final Test Metrics**:
  * **Pairwise Ranking Accuracy**: `90.51%`
  * **ROC AUC**: `0.8397`
* **Checkpoint Saved**: `models/makes_sense_tinystories.pt`

---

## 5. Policy Head
Trained to predict survival likelihood during search to prune candidate transitions early.
* **Architecture**: 3-layer MLP (`input_dim=512`, `hidden=[256, 64]`, `dropout=0.2`) taking the concatenated context/candidate embeddings.
* **Dataset**: Generated by running 35 beam search runs on the TinyStories corpus.
  * Train: 19,600 samples
  * Val: 2,450 samples
  * Test: 2,450 samples
* **Training Hyperparameters**:
  * Epochs: 20 (Early stopping saved epoch 19 best)
  * Learning Rate: 1e-3
  * Batch Size: 64
* **Final Test Metrics**:
  * **Accuracy**: `96.94%`
  * **Precision**: `37.50%`
  * **Recall**: `4.11%`
  * **F1 Score**: `0.0741`
  * **ROC AUC**: `0.7378`
* **Interpretation of Metrics**:
  * Because the target positive class (surviving transition) is extremely sparse in beam search (~0.6% positive ratio), the dataset is highly imbalanced. The high overall Accuracy and moderate ROC AUC show that the model learns a strong ranking representation suitable for sorting candidates, even though the raw F1 is low.
* **Checkpoint Saved**: `models/policy_tinystories.pt`

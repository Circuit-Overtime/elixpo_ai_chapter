# Governance

The lead maintainer is @Circuit-Overtime. Material scope, research-claim,
release, governance, and lifecycle changes require maintainer review. Paper
authorship and artifact attribution follow the underlying contribution record
and are not inferred from repository membership.

Contributors retain attribution for accepted work. Repository transfer,
licensing, security disclosure, publication, and project-status decisions
require explicit human approval. Canonical public status is recorded at
[elixpo.com](https://elixpo.com).

LixBlogs is a publishing platform for individual creators and organizations. You can draft rich stories, collaborate with co-authors, publish under your profile or an organization, and understand how readers find and engage with your work.

## The publishing workflow

1. Choose **Write** and create a story.
2. Add a title, subtitle, topics, cover, and content.
3. Use **Preview** to check the public layout.
4. Open the publish panel to choose the slug, visibility, author, organization, or collection.
5. Publish the story. Later updates return you to its public page.

Drafts remain private to their owner and accepted collaborators. Published stories can be public, unlisted, or secret depending on the visibility selected by the owner.

## Profiles, organizations, and collections

- Your **profile** contains your published stories, followers, and badges you choose to display.
- An **organization** is a shared publication. Its roles determine who can publish and manage it.
- A **collection** groups related organization stories into a browsable series.

## Where to manage your account

Use **Settings** to update your creator preferences, inspect media usage, view analytics, and manage connected services. Identity details such as your username originate from [Elixpo Accounts](https://accounts.elixpo.com/).

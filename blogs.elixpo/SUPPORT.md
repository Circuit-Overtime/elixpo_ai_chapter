# Support

LixBlogs is maintained in public without a guaranteed support SLA.

## Where to ask

- Reproducible bug: [open an issue](https://github.com/elixpo/blogs.elixpo/issues/new).
- Feature or product discussion: [Elixpo Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/elixpo/discussions).
- Contribution question: read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md), then use an issue or discussion.
- Security or privacy vulnerability: follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); do not post it publicly.

Include the affected URL or package, expected and actual behavior, minimal
reproduction, runtime/browser versions, and relevant logs with credentials and
personal data removed.

Account recovery, moderation appeals, billing, and hosted-service data requests
cannot be resolved through public issues. Use the contact route published by
the relevant operated service.

Forks and self-hosted deployments are welcome, but their operators own their
infrastructure, data, upgrades, and support obligations.

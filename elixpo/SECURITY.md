# Security policy

## Reporting

Report vulnerabilities through GitHub's private vulnerability-reporting flow
for `elixpo/elixpo` when available. If the issue belongs to another Elixpo
project, use that repository's security policy. Do not include credentials,
personal data, private infrastructure details, or working exploit material in
a public issue or discussion.

Include the affected route or component, impact, reproduction conditions, and
the smallest safe evidence needed to evaluate the report.

## Scope and expectations

The actively maintained portal source and its publication workflows are in
scope. Archived repositories and third-party services linked from the portal
are governed by their own policies. Submission does not guarantee a bounty,
embargo period, response deadline, or acceptance of the reported severity.

Maintainers will coordinate disclosure proportionately to the demonstrated
risk and affected users.

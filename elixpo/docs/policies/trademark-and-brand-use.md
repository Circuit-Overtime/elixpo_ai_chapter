# Elixpo trademark and brand-use policy

**Status:** Published operating policy  
**Effective:** 2026-10-17  
**Machine-readable source:** [`content/licensing.json`](../../content/licensing.json)

This policy explains when Elixpo names and visual marks may be used. It is
separate from the copyright license on source code, documentation, research,
hardware, or other artifacts. An open-source license does not grant ownership
of an Elixpo identity or permission to imply Elixpo endorsement.

## Accurate referential use

You may use the Elixpo name, and an unmodified public logo where useful, to:

- identify a genuine Elixpo repository, service, package, research record, or
  contribution;
- link to an official Elixpo page or explain accurate compatibility;
- describe a documented event partnership within its approved scope; or
- reproduce a factual attribution already present in the public record.

The surrounding text must make the relationship clear. Independent and Member
Projects must not be presented as owned, certified, or operated by Elixpo when
the registry says otherwise.

## Uses requiring written permission

Ask Elixpo before using a mark for official sticker production, merchandise,
event co-branding, a modified logo, a product or organization name, a domain or
account that may appear official, or any promotion likely to imply sponsorship,
certification, or governance authority. Event permissions are bounded by the
written partnership record and do not transfer ownership of the artwork.

Official sticker masters and print sheets are controlled partnership material.
Watermarked previews are not print licenses and may not be reproduced,
distributed, modified, or sold.

## Uses that are not permitted

Do not use Elixpo marks to impersonate the organization, create deceptive
affiliation, resell official artwork, register confusing official-looking
identities, or claim that Elixpo approved a project, person, event, product, or
research conclusion when it did not.

## Open design guidance

Published colour tokens, typography guidance, geometric principles, and general
usage guidance remain available for reference. Their publication does not make
every asset an unrestricted trademark or copyright grant. The artifact's own
license and this identity boundary both apply.

## Corrections and permission requests

Use the contact route on [elixpo.com/partnerships](https://elixpo.com/partnerships)
for permission requests, or open a portal issue for a factual correction. Elixpo
may ask that misleading use be corrected or stopped. Nothing here limits uses
that applicable law permits independently of this policy.

This is an operational publication policy, not legal advice.

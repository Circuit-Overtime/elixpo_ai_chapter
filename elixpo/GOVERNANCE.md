# Governance

The Elixpo portal and canonical registry are maintainer-led. Elixpo maintainers
own organization-level classification, policy, publishing, and operational
decisions. Project maintainers retain authority over their own repositories and
artifacts; a portal record does not transfer that authority.

Changes to lifecycle, custody, licensing evidence, research claims,
contributor attribution, sponsorship policy, or repository relationships must
enter through a reviewable pull request with primary evidence. Automated checks
may detect drift and prepare updates, but they do not make these decisions.

The default branch uses squash-only pull requests, resolved review threads,
required deterministic checks, and no mandatory approval count by current
maintainer decision. Privileged deployment and cross-repository credentials
remain limited to their documented workflows.

Questions and proposed changes belong in
[GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/elixpo/discussions). Security
reports must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

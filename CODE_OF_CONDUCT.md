# Code of Conduct

Crumb follows the Elixpo community standard: participation must be respectful,
constructive, and safe for contributors with different backgrounds and levels
of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss technical decisions without attacking people.
- Give actionable feedback and acknowledge the work behind a contribution.
- Respect privacy, attribution, authorship, and stated boundaries.
- Avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, sustained
  disruption, or publication of another person's private information.
- Treat agent-generated output as the responsibility of the person submitting
  it; automation does not excuse harmful or low-integrity behavior.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `hello@elixpo.com`. Do not open a public
issue containing sensitive details. Elixpo maintainers may edit or remove
content, close participation paths, temporarily restrict access, or permanently
exclude a participant when needed to protect the community.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and made
without retaliation against good-faith reporters. Project maintainers may
escalate organization-wide concerns to the Elixpo leads.

This policy applies in repository spaces, Elixpo discussions, project events,
and public interactions where a person is representing Crumb or Elixpo.

# Elixpo sponsorship policy

**Status:** Elixpo is not accepting organization sponsorships  
**Last verified:** 2026-10-16  
**Machine-readable source:** [`content/sponsorship.json`](../../content/sponsorship.json)

Elixpo does not currently solicit, accept, hold, or route organization-level
sponsorship funds. It has not designated a legal recipient or adopted the
accounting and reporting process required to handle such funds responsibly.

Organization READMEs, websites, project directories, partnership requests, and
shared contribution material must not link to an individual founder's funding
account as though it were an Elixpo funding destination.

## Project-owned funding

An independently governed project may publish its own funding route under its
own authority and accounting. That route belongs to the project and must name
the actual recipient. Listing the project through Elixpo does not make Elixpo
the recipient, intermediary, guarantor, or endorser.

Project-owned funding links belong on the project's dossier or repository, not
in the shared organization brief.

## Independence

Money or in-kind support does not purchase:

- Chapter selection, Member Project status, or feature placement;
- favourable editorial treatment;
- control of research conclusions or review labels;
- contributor credit or governance authority; or
- an exception to licensing, security, review, or conduct requirements.

Past support may be acknowledged only as a dated, evidence-backed historical
fact. Acknowledgement must not imply an ongoing relationship or endorsement.

## Stickers, brand material, and event partnerships

Official stickers are sponsor-controlled event material. The public asset
library provides visibly watermarked previews only. A preview is not a
print-ready source and does not grant permission to reproduce, print,
distribute, modify, or sell the artwork.

Elixpo may approve an **in-kind event partnership** that releases a physical
sticker pack or print-ready artwork for the agreed event in exchange for
disclosed community visibility. Approval and the permitted use are recorded in
writing before files or stock are released. No money is accepted or routed
through this model.

- For up to 100 stickers, the event provides community-partner credit and a
  linked social mention.
- For more than 100 stickers, those credits remain and the event provides an
  on-stage slot of no more than ten minutes for an Elixpo introduction.
- Around 200 stickers or more requires a written custom agreement before
  production. The ten-minute introduction remains; any additional visibility
  or venue space must be agreed for that event rather than assumed here.

Requests must identify the event, date, venue, organizers, public event page,
expected attendance, requested quantity, delivery deadline and location,
printing responsibility, distribution plan, proposed visibility, and the
organizer responsible for confirming delivery and outcomes.

The public explanation and request route live at
[`elixpo.com/partnerships`](https://elixpo.com/partnerships). Elixpo's colour
tokens, typography guidance, and general brand guidance remain openly
accessible in the [brand library](https://elixpo.com/branding). This access
boundary does not settle the broader copyright or trademark policy, which
remains a separate legal-review task.

## Reopening sponsorship later

Elixpo must not accept organization sponsorship until a reviewed policy names:

- the legal recipient;
- the accounting and public reporting process;
- conflict-of-interest and editorial-independence rules;
- the outcomes support may fund and what it cannot purchase; and
- the person or body accountable for approving and reporting expenditure.

Any future change must update the canonical machine-readable policy before a
public funding link is added.

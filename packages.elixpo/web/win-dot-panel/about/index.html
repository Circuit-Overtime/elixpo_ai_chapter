About Win Dot Panel

A familiar little shortcut for Linux desktops.

Win Dot Panel brings emoji, symbols, kaomoji, and useful clipboard history into one calm panel built for everyday keyboard use.

The idea

Windows and macOS make common characters easy to reach without interrupting your work. Linux offers powerful individual tools, but the experience varies between desktops and display systems. Win Dot Panel brings the useful pieces together with shortcuts that are easy to remember.

What matters

Local first

Emoji search and clipboard history work without sending your content to an online service.

Keyboard friendly

Open the exact tab you need, search immediately, and return to your work.

Desktop aware

Supports GNOME and KDE Plasma across modern Wayland and X11 sessions.

Open source

Application code, tests, packaging, and release automation are public.

Your clipboard stays yours

Clipboard history is stored in a database under your own Linux user account. Win Dot Panel does not require an account and does not upload clipboard contents. Anyone with access to your Linux account may still be able to read your local files, so use normal device security for sensitive systems.

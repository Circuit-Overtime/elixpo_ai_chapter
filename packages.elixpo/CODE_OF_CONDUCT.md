# Code of Conduct

Elixpo is committed to a respectful, harassment-free environment for everyone
participating in its repositories and community spaces. Be constructive,
specific, patient, and considerate of different backgrounds and experience.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sustained disruption, and publication of
private information are not accepted. Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or
remove material and restrict participation when necessary to protect the
community.

Report conduct concerns privately to `conduct@elixpo.com`. Do not use public
issues for sensitive reports. Enforcement decisions follow the Elixpo
organization’s published community policies.
